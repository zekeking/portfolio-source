
Critterdex aims to capture the fun of going to large social events and having asynreonous encounters, with users "tagging" one another wwhen they are in local proximity to one another. It moves away from online only play and instead incorporates a hybrid technology for a collectathon style social game. Players get out of these encounters stickers that can be used to complete sets or duplicates that can be traded in for mini games. 

The technology that Critterdex is built on is Android. It uses SQLite for its database. Hosting is done through Firebase.

The design around monetization balances carefully with the gameplay loop. It aims to be freemium, with positive experience being a motivating factor for supporting the game as well as providing oppurtunities for prolific collectors to add additional item space to their inventory. Cosmetics to further personalize the avatar used in encounters is another friendly way players support development.
